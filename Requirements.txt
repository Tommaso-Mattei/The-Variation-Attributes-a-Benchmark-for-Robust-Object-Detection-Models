Requirements:

If the goal is to use the benchmark on your own model output then you need:

PyTorch
Torchvision
OpenCV
Matplotlib

If additionally you want to recreate the conditions of the experiments with Faster R-CNN, YOLO11 and RT-DETR then you also need:

Numpy
GluonCV
Ultralytics

My own experiments were made on the following versions:

python==3.14.7
torch==2.13.0+cu130
cv2==5.0.0
torchvision==0.28.0+cu130
matplotlib==3.11.1
numpy==2.3.5
ultralytics==8.4.126
gluoncv==0.11.0

GluonCV requires a different environment to be deployed in to avoid version conflicts (python==3.7.16). Used only to deploy pre-trained Faster R-CNN on Pascal VOC.
